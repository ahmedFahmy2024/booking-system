# Cinema booking

Customers reserve specific seats for cinema showtimes. Cinema staff manage the programme and its bookings.

## Language

**Cinema branch**  
A physical cinema location that contains one or more auditoriums.

**Auditorium**  
A screening room at a cinema branch with its own seating layout.

**Film**  
The title shown at one or more showtimes.

**Showtime**  
A scheduled screening of a film in one auditorium.

**Seat**  
A numbered or labelled place in an auditorium. Its availability is evaluated for a particular showtime.

**Booking**  
A reservation of one or more seats for a single showtime.

**Guest customer**  
A person who makes a booking using an email address without creating an account.

**Counter sale**  
A ticket sale initiated by staff at the cinema box office.

**Booking reference**  
The identifier a guest can quote to cinema staff when discussing a booking.

**Guest booking link**  
The private link emailed to a guest to open their booking details and access permitted actions.

**Seat selection**  
The seats a guest has picked on the showtime seat map before confirming the booking. Other customers can still book them.

**No-show**  
A confirmed booking whose guest has not arrived by the start of the showtime and which staff mark as unattended.

**Admin**  
A staff member responsible for managing staff access, films, seating layouts, showtimes, and prices.

**Operations staff**  
A staff member responsible for handling existing bookings, guest arrivals, and payments at the cinema.

**Showtime cancellation**  
The withdrawal of a scheduled screening and the end of its active bookings. The records remain available to staff.
